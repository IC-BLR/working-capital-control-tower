Policy ID: REG-NEW-010
Title: Material Invoice Approval Policy
Category: Audit
Jurisdiction: India
Approval Threshold: USD 50000
REG-001: Invoices must have purchase order linkage unless explicitly approved as Non-PO.
REG-002: All invoices above threshold require Finance Manager approval.
REG-003: GST and TDS evidence must be reviewed by Tax Reviewer.
REG-004: Missing contract or rate card mismatch must block payment and create exception.
REG-005: Controller approval is required for high-risk or override payments.
